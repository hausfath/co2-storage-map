Global CO₂ Storage Atlas

A map of where the world can store a stream of CO₂, either in sedimentary basins by injecting it into saline aquifers and depleted fields, or in reactive rock, through in-situ mineralization (ISM). Sedimentary storage will likely remain cheaper, but in many parts of the world it isn't an option, and mineralization is. Carbon removal at scale needs a diversity of storage sites around the globe.
