Global CO₂ Storage Atlas

Where the world can store CO₂: in sedimentary basins, by injecting it into saline aquifers and depleted fields, and in reactive rock, by in-situ mineralization (ISM). Sedimentary storage will likely remain cheaper, but in many parts of the world it isn't an option, and mineralization is. Carbon removal at scale needs a diversity of storage sites around the globe.
