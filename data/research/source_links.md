# Source hyperlinks — method, coverage and review list (2026-09)

Citation strings in `basin_capacity.json`, `sedimentary_capacity.json`, `mineralization_capacity.json`
and `projects.json` are free text. Rather than editing hundreds of strings, links are applied at
render time: `source_links.json` maps exact citation substrings to URLs (longest match wins), and the
app's `linkify()` wraps those matches plus any raw URL/domain in the text. Projects carry a `url`
field rendered as "Project page".

## How links were obtained
Six Sonnet research agents looked up each distinct citation / project and were required to fetch
every URL and confirm it is the cited document before marking it verified. Merged by
`merge_links.py`; only `verified` entries were applied automatically. Of the remaining candidates,
those that (a) returned HTTP 200 to a direct request from this machine and (b) are unambiguously the
cited document (OGCI CSRC Cycle 5 main report, resolving DOIs, CO2CRC's own Otway page) were added
with `existence_checked_only: true`; placeholders such as a publisher homepage were rejected.
